# Form 8854 Filing Deadline: When and How to File Your Expatriation Statement

Form 8854 is the expatriation information statement used to report covered-expatriate tests and related section 877A items. The filing obligation and timing depend on the expatriation year and post-expatriation items.

## When to File

Form 8854 is due by the due date of your **tax return for the year of expatriation**:

| Situation | Due Date |
|---|---|
| Standard filer | April 15 of the following year |
| With extension | October 15 of the following year |
| Taxpayer living abroad | Apply the current abroad extension rules, then any valid additional extension |

## Determining Your Expatriation Date

### Citizenship Renunciation
- The expatriation date for a citizenship renunciation generally follows the legal loss-of-nationality record; confirm the date on the Certificate of Loss of Nationality and the current tax instructions.
- The consular process, fee, and immigration record are separate from the federal tax filing analysis.

### Green Card Abandonment
- For a green-card abandonment, the relevant date generally follows the legal abandonment record, often involving **Form I-407**; confirm the facts and long-term-resident history.
- You can file Form I-407 at a US embassy or consulate or by mail to USCIS.
- **Long-term residents** (green card holders for 8 of the last 15 years) are subject to the exit tax, just like citizens.

## What Form 8854 Requires

### Part I: Identification and Covered Expatriate Status
- Personal information.
- Certification of five-year tax compliance.
- The three tests (income tax, net worth, compliance).

### Part II: Deemed Sale Calculation (Covered Expatriates Only)
- List of all assets.
- FMV, basis, and gain/loss for each.
- Application of the year-specific net-gain exclusion; the 2025 instructions use $890,000.

### Part III: Deferred Compensation and Trusts
- Identification of deferred compensation items.
- Trust interest reporting.
- Waiver of treaty benefits.

## Extensions

- **Automatic abroad extension:** If the IRS conditions apply, the income-tax return may receive the automatic two-month filing extension.
- **Additional extension:** Follow the current Form 4868 and return instructions for an additional extension.
- **Form 8854 timing:** The initial form follows the due date of the return to which it is attached; annual post-expatriation filings can have their own continuing context.

## Penalties

- **$10,000** for a required form that is not timely, complete, and accurate, unless the reasonable-cause exception applies.
- Failure to certify five years of federal tax compliance can make a person a covered expatriate under the statutory test.
- Other assessment and reporting consequences depend on the missing item and tax year.
