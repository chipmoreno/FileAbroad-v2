# UK SIPPs for US Taxpayers: Trust Reporting, Treaty Benefits, and PFIC Risks

The UK Self-Invested Personal Pension (SIPP) is a UK retirement arrangement whose U.S. treatment depends on the plan documents, taxpayer status, treaty provisions, contributions, distributions, and investments. For Americans living in the UK—or British citizens who later become U.S. taxpayers—the correct review starts with the actual SIPP terms rather than a product-label assumption.

## What Is a UK SIPP?

A SIPP is a UK-registered pension scheme that allows the member to choose their own investments. Unlike a workplace pension with limited fund options, a SIPP can hold:
- Stocks and shares.
- Unit trusts and OEICs.
- Investment trusts.
- Commercial property.
- Cash.

## US Tax Treatment: The Trust Problem

U.S. tax deferral and treaty treatment are not automatic from the product name. The analysis starts with the SIPP's legal structure, the taxpayer's status, and the applicable treaty and domestic-law rules.

### Grantor Trust Analysis

Some U.S. analyses may treat a SIPP as a **foreign trust or grantor-trust arrangement**, but the result is not a published blanket rule for every SIPP. Review:
- The plan deed and governing law.
- Who owns and controls the assets under U.S. tax principles.
- Contributions, employer funding, investment control, and distributions.
- Treaty provisions and any applicable retirement-account or trust exceptions.

### Implications of Trust Treatment

If the facts support foreign-grantor-trust treatment and a filing trigger applies:
- **Form 3520:** May be relevant for reportable transfers, ownership, or distributions.
- **Form 3520-A:** May be relevant for a foreign trust with a U.S. owner; the filing instructions and exceptions control.
- **Income inclusion:** The U.S. tax treatment of growth and distributions depends on the plan and applicable law.
- **PFIC rules:** If the SIPP holds PFIC stock and a Form 8621 filing trigger applies, Form 8621 may be required.

### The US-UK Tax Treaty

The U.S.-UK treaty's pension provisions may affect the treatment of pension income, but the result depends on the relevant treaty text, protocols, residence, saving clause, and whether the arrangement qualifies as a pension scheme. Treaty treatment of a distribution does not automatically answer trust, PFIC, or information-reporting questions during accumulation.

## PFICs Inside SIPPs

This is where many US taxpayers get blindsided.

### Common PFICs in SIPPs
- Vanguard UK-listed ETFs (e.g., VUKE, VWRL, VHYL).
- iShares European ETFs.
- UK unit trusts and OEICs.
- Global equity funds domiciled in Ireland or Luxembourg.

### Lower-PFIC-Risk Screening Examples
- **Individual UK stocks:** Often lower PFIC screening risk than a pooled foreign fund, but the issuer still must be analyzed.
- **Direct bonds:** UK government bonds (gilts), corporate bonds held directly.
- **Cash:** Generally not PFIC stock, but account reporting can still apply.
- **U.S.-domiciled ETFs:** Generally not PFICs themselves if the legal domicile is confirmed.

### Strategy for US Taxpayers with SIPPs

1. **Inventory the holdings:** Record each fund's legal issuer, domicile, and tax documents.
2. **Screen pooled foreign funds:** Irish-, Luxembourg-, and UK-domiciled funds are high-priority PFIC review items, not automatic classifications.
3. **Ask the provider about alternatives:** Confirm whether direct stock or U.S.-domiciled fund options are available and whether a sale creates U.S. or UK tax.
4. **Model any transfer:** A self-directed option may change investment choice but does not by itself settle the U.S. reporting position.

## Form 3520 Filing for SIPPs

### Contributions
- Employer contributions, employee contributions, and UK tax relief may need to be analyzed as plan contributions or transfers under the applicable U.S. rules.
- Do not treat the HMRC "relief at source" mechanism as an automatic Form 3520 filing result.

### Distributions
- UK access rules and any tax-free amount are separate from the U.S. tax result.
- The U.S. may not mirror a UK exemption or tax-free treatment, and the treaty may change the result.
- Classify the distribution under the U.S. rules and the applicable treaty before assuming ordinary-income, capital-gain, or exempt treatment.

### Annual Reporting
- If Form 3520-A applies, the current IRS instructions specify the foreign trust's annual information and owner statements.
- A provider's statement may not contain every item needed for a U.S. filing, so collect plan and investment records early.
